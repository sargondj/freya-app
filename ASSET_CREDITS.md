# Public Asset Credits

The three nature buttons use real recordings at their original pitch and speed.
All app audio is served from this repository, without external players or ads.

## Owl

- App file: `audio/yellow-owl.wav` (10.2 seconds).
- Recording: **Bubo virginianus - Great Horned Owl XC156403**, by **Bushman**, recorded December 2, 2013, Salem, Oregon.
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bubo_virginianus_-_Great_Horned_Owl_XC156403.mp3
- Original recording information: https://xeno-canto.org/156403
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
- This edited audio file is also distributed under **CC BY-SA 3.0**.
- Edits: excerpt from 14.2 to 24.4 seconds; mono 44.1 kHz PCM WAV; 220 Hz high-pass to reduce rumble; 1.6 kHz low-pass; brief start/end fades; peak normalized to 70% of full scale. No synthesized calls, pitch shift, or speed change.

## Whale

- App file: `audio/monterey-humpback-song.mp3` (24 seconds), for the blue button. The animal is a humpback whale.
- Recording: **humpback whale song**, recorded December 7, 2015, by the Monterey Bay Aquarium Research Institute (MBARI) using its MARS hydrophone off the California coast.
- Source and license: https://freesound.org/people/MBARI_MARS/sounds/448984/ (Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/).
- Downloaded source: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/448/448984_6181234-hq.mp3 (public high-quality MP3 preview).
- Edited excerpt distributed under CC BY-NC 4.0. Edits: 4 to 28 seconds, mono, 44.1 kHz MP3, 75 Hz high-pass, 4 kHz low-pass, volume normalization, and brief fades. Natural song retains its original pitch and speed.
- This recording is from the same organization and Monterey Bay hydrophone project as the [video requested by the caregiver](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5tRMqbPH_pk). It is a separately published recording; the video's exact audio was not extracted because its reuse license was not identified.

## Wind Chimes

- App file: `audio/green-wind-chimes.wav` (16 seconds).
- Recording: **Big_Chimes_LR.WAV**, by **EELLC**, February 8, 2015. Real large backyard wind chimes recorded with a Zoom H6.
- Source: https://freesound.org/people/EELLC/sounds/263613/
- Downloaded source: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/263/263613_4935365-hq.mp3 (public high-quality MP3 preview).
- License: [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- Edits: first 16 seconds; mono 44.1 kHz PCM WAV; 100 Hz high-pass and 6 kHz low-pass; short attack fade and 1.5-second ending fade; peak normalized to 70%. No pitch or speed change.

## Illustrations And Purple Demo

The owl, humpback whale, and tubular wind-chime SVG illustrations were drawn specifically for this app. They are original illustrations, not downloaded stock assets. They emphasize identifying features while keeping outlines simple. These three replacement SVGs are dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The crown icon and original `pink-sparkle.wav` demo are unchanged. The purple button still supports a file stored privately on the individual device.

## Verification Limits

Source descriptions, waveforms, frequency content, peak levels, and browser decoding were inspected. Subjective listening was not available in the development session. A caregiver should audition all three clips on the intended device before Freya uses them, especially to confirm her preferred whale pitch and chime tone.
